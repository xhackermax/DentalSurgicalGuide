DSG 9.1.21 — Geodesic Safe Surface Anchors
================================================

Problem
-------
The width-gated frame engine correctly rejected a tube when a contour anchor
lay on a surface zone too narrow/steep for the requested diameter. But it treated
the raw clicked point as immutable and aborted the complete frame.

Worse, after validating a graph endpoint it restored the exact raw click to the
final polyline, which could reintroduce the invalid local width.

New policy
----------
A contour click is semantic intent.

requested_anchor
    -> nearest passive-model graph vertex
    -> local full-diameter width test
    -> if invalid: Dijkstra-like bounded neighbourhood walk on actual mesh edges
    -> closest width-valid surface vertex
    -> resolved_anchor

Important guarantees
--------------------
- relocation search follows mesh edges (surface-geodesic distance)
- no KD/Euclidean jump across teeth, embrasures or concavities
- no Euclidean frame fallback
- final full route is still Dijkstra on the passive surface
- final complete route is still hard full-diameter validated
- automatic relocation is capped at 4.50 mm geodesically
- if no valid endpoint exists inside that cap, DSG fails closed
- the final frame uses the resolved endpoint and does NOT restore the bad click
