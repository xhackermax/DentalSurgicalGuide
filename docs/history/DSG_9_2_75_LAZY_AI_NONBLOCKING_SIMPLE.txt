DSG v9.2.75 — Lazy AI Worker + Nonblocking SIMPLE Route

Root cause of v9.2.74 slowdown:
1. CBCT_READY launched TWO heavy activities automatically:
   - foreground Blender deep verification imported Torch/nnU-Net in a Python
     thread, still sharing Blender's process/GIL/import lock;
   - persistent background Blender simultaneously pre-warmed both AI models.
   CPU, RAM, disk and GPU pressure therefore started before a route was chosen.
2. SIMPLE_DENSITY also scheduled a solid threshold preview. The timer eventually
   executed its mesh build on Blender's main thread, causing the delayed grey
   / Not Responding freeze reported after choosing Implante simple.

v9.2.75:
- Patient opening performs zero Torch imports and zero CUDA/model warmup.
- The persistent AI process starts only after IMPLANTE INMEDIATO is explicitly
  chosen.
- Immediate route availability uses only cheap structural/checkpoint checks.
- The first worker request does NOT prewarm both models before inference.
  DentalSegmentator loads on demand, remains cached, then UniversalLab loads in
  the same persistent process.
- SIMPLE route force-stops any old AI worker to return GPU/RAM to Blender.
- SIMPLE threshold interaction is GPU/OpenVDB volume-only.
- No delayed solid preview is scheduled while adjusting DENSIDAD/HU.
- Full-resolution STL generation remains unchanged and starts only after
  SIGUIENTE/confirm.
- No segmentation accuracy or CBCT resolution is reduced.
