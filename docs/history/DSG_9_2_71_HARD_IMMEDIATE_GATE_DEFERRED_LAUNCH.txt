DSG v9.2.71

Fix for Blender freezing / grey "Not Responding" window when IMPLANTE INMEDIATO
is pressed prematurely after patient/CBCT loading.

Changes:
1. Every new CBCT invalidates the immediate-route gate.
2. CBCT_READY stores a fresh wall-clock generation timestamp.
3. Runtime deep verification is forced after every CBCT load (force=True).
4. A stale verification result from addon startup or another patient cannot arm
   IMPLANTE INMEDIATO.
5. The button requires the current verification to finish AFTER this CBCT ready
   timestamp, plus a 1.25 s UI-idle settling barrier.
6. The operator poll and execute both enforce the same gate.
7. Clicking IMPLANTE INMEDIATO only publishes IMMEDIATE_LAUNCHING and returns.
   The heavy segmentation operator is started 250 ms later by bpy.app.timers,
   after Blender has repainted the disabled UI and released the mouse event.
8. Double-click/re-entry remains blocked.
