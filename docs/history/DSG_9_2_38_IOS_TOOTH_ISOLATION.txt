DSG 9.2.38 - Immediate-extraction IOS tooth isolation

The common-surface matcher is now explicitly an extraction-tooth isolation
step, not merely an overlap visualisation.

- The selected FDI tooth/teeth segmented from CBCT are the only references.
- The aligned IOS copy is the only editable target.
- One selected FDI retains one connected IOS tooth island; multiple FDI values
  retain the corresponding limited number of connected islands.
- Nearby interproximal matches cannot create additional retained islands for a
  single-tooth extraction.
- The clinician refines only the cervical/contact boundary with Ctrl+.
- On confirmation, DSG deletes that selected IOS tooth island, closes its
  boundary, then performs the existing CBCT socket Boolean and closed-model
  cleanup protocol.

Neither IOS nor CBCT source geometry is moved, scaled or edited during common
surface selection.
