DSG 9.1.20 — Full Mandibular Canal Ensemble
=================================================

Problem corrected
-----------------
DSG 9.1.19 converted the thin class-55 canal mask to mesh through a generic
max_axis=640 sampling path. If the compact canal ROI exceeded 640 voxels in any
axis, stride > 1 could erase 1-2 voxel segments and visibly truncate the canal.
It also filtered small connected components before meshing.

New pipeline
------------
SEGMENTAR TODO remains one visible button, but internally:

1. DentalSegmentator (bundled Dataset112)
   - class 5 = Mandibular canal
   - full prediction is temporary
   - only compact class-5 canal mask is retained

2. UniversalLab
   - teeth / FDI / maxilla / mandible
   - class 55 = mandibular canal
   - remains DSG dental semantic SSOT

3. Canal ensemble
   - UniversalLab 55 is preserved
   - full DentalSegmentator canal components are accepted when they overlap or
     approach UniversalLab, plus at most the two largest substantial components
     for bilateral recovery
   - no long path is synthesised
   - only <=0.65 mm morphological closing is allowed for microscopic gaps

4. Mesh
   - native compact-mask resolution
   - stride = 1 always
   - no pre-mesh downsampling
   - no generic component deletion
   - no destructive smoothing/remeshing

Safety
------
This object represents the learned bony mandibular canal, used as a proxy for
the inferior alveolar neurovascular bundle. It is not a direct MRI
segmentation of the nerve itself. Model disagreement and missing canal segments
must remain visible rather than being fabricated geometrically.
