DSG 9.2.35 - Blockout seating audit

New guide-fit safeguards

- The view-parallel blockout is now audited automatically against the evaluated IOS.
- DSG verifies equal source/candidate topology, axial-only displacement and
  sampled residual axial locks before the blockout can be confirmed as the
  fitting model.
- The added blockout area is exposed as a teal, display-only review overlay.
- The panel presents a direct seating state: passed, review required or failed.
- "Recheck seating" repeats the audit without modifying the patient model.
- Blockout generation and confirmation participate in Blender Undo.

Clinical scope

This is an algorithmic geometry quality control. It does not certify a perfect
clinical fit, replace printed-guide verification, compensate for acquisition or
printing errors, or replace the clinician's pre-surgical trial seating.

Design references

- Blender for Dental, "The Flipper Nobody Respects: Designing a One-Tooth
  Partial Denture That Actually Seats". Used as interaction/design inspiration
  for an insertion-axis survey and local relief review, not as a direct
  parameter source for surgical guides.
- Blue Sky Plan User Manual, Surgical guides section. Confirms the established
  digital-guide pattern of an offset surface derived from the oral model.
- Tahmaseb et al., Clinical Oral Implants Research (2018), systematic review
  of static computer-aided implant surgery. Guide design does not remove the
  need for safety margins and clinical verification.
