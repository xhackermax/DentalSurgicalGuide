DSG v9.2.74 — Persistent Warm AI Worker

Architecture:
- A single Blender --background process starts automatically after a patient
  CBCT reaches CBCT_READY.
- It remains alive until DSG is disabled/Blender closes.
- Torch/nnU-Net are imported once.
- UniversalLab and DentalSegmentator are pre-warmed. Up to two predictors stay
  resident when memory permits; adaptive fallback retains one if VRAM is tight.
- The first immediate-route model (DentalSegmentator) is deliberately warmed
  last so it remains resident on one-model GPUs.
- Semantic and Universal jobs are sent to the same warm process over localhost
  with a random authentication token.
- Large CBCT arrays are written once into a patient cache and reused by the
  second engine, instead of re-serializing the scan between semantic/universal.
- Progress travels over the local socket while Blender's foreground process
  remains interactive.
- No downsampling, preview mask or alternative inference algorithm is used.
- Existing isolated one-shot process and in-process thread remain compatibility
  fallbacks when a persistent worker cannot be launched.
