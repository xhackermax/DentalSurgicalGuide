DSG v9.2.72

Immediate-implant final animation now starts with the extracted tooth animation:
1. DSG duplicates the virtually extracted tooth (or immediate-extraction FDI set).
2. The duplicate moves coronally/incisally along its estimated long-axis extraction direction.
3. The direction is forced root -> crown by comparing cross-sectional width at both ends, so the motion never goes apically.
4. The extracted tooth then disappears.
5. Only after that does the pre-existing final sequence begin unchanged: guide seating, screws, drill/implant pairs, reverse screw removal, and guide retraction.

Other notes:
- The original hidden tooth remains untouched.
- Sequence bounds now include the extraction prelude.
- Rebuilding the sequence first clears any previous animated extraction duplicates.
