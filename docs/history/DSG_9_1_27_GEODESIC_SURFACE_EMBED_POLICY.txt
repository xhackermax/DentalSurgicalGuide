DSG 9.1.27 — Geodesic Surface Embed Policy
============================================

The geodesic centreline remains 100% on the passive-model mesh. No Euclidean
fallback is introduced.

The width validator samples both sides at 50%, 75%, and 100% of tube radius.
A conflict only at 100% is accepted, allowing the outer 25% radial band to
overlap/embed into the support model. A conflict at 75% or 50% remains a hard
bottleneck and triggers intrinsic Dijkstra rerouting.

This changes the former HARD_FULL_DIAMETER all-or-nothing rule into:
    GEODESIC_SURFACE_EMBED_25_PERCENT

It applies consistently to contour anchors and every route sample.
The generated object stores the minimum retained ratio, allowed embed ratio and
number of samples that used the tolerance.
