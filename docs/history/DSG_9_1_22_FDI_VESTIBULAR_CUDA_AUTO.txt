DSG 9.1.22 — FDI vestibular + CUDA AUTO
=============================================

FDI visualisation
-----------------
Previous 9.1.17+ logic had three weaknesses visible in real Mixar testing:
1. label anchor was chosen mainly from the most coronal tooth point, causing
   maxillary and mandibular labels to crowd the occlusal/inter-arch space.
2. orientation followed a tooth-derived frame but did not explicitly face the
   vestibular surface, so some numbers appeared edge-on or rotated.
3. label objects were parented to DICOM_ROOT, producing long Blender relationship
   lines converging near the dataset origin.

9.1.22:
- builds every tooth first, then computes each complete arch centre.
- vestibular direction = radial direction away from that arch centre, projected
  perpendicular to the learned tooth long axis.
- anchor = most vestibular vertex restricted to the coronal half of the tooth,
  plus 0.55 mm outward offset.
- glyph normal faces vestibular.
- glyph vertical axis follows patient superior (+Z in DICOM LPS world space).
- compact 2.15 mm label size.
- each label is parented to its tooth, not DICOM_ROOT.
- manual FDI edits, arch swaps and tooth deletion automatically rebuild layout.

CUDA
----
Immediately after a valid DICOM is selected:
- DSG imports its installed PyTorch runtime once.
- queries torch.cuda.is_available().
- on multi-GPU NVIDIA systems chooses the adapter with most free VRAM.
- stores GPU name and free/total VRAM.
- sets ai_device_preference = CUDA when CUDA is usable.
- otherwise selects CPU.
- NVIDIA-driver + CPU-only Torch is reported as a runtime repair condition.

Cycles CUDA/OptiX settings remain unrelated; DSG segmentation uses PyTorch.
