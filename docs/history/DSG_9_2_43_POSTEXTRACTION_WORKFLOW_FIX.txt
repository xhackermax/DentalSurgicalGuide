DSG 9.2.43 - Immediate post-extraction IOS workflow repair

Problem

The immediate route contained the required common-surface matcher and the
anatomy-safe model-closure functions, but the Alignment panel still presented
CONTINUE immediately after a successful 3-zone registration. That contradicted
the clinical workflow because the post-extraction IOS had not yet been created.
The crown-deletion step also allowed the Boolean stage to continue even when the
local IOS opening could not be verified as closed.

Deterministic workflow

1. Clinician selects the extraction tooth/teeth in the segmented CBCT.
2. DSG creates the alignment composite without those teeth; the original
   segmented extraction teeth remain available as hidden geometric references.
3. IOS is aligned and the three-zone validation must pass.
4. DETECT COMMON SURFACE uses only the selected segmented extraction teeth as
   references and selects their corresponding unsegmented crown surface on a
   disposable aligned-IOS copy.
5. Clinician reviews/refines the cervical/contact boundary in Edit Mode.
6. APPLY EXTRACTION + CLOSE performs, transactionally where possible:
   - deletion of the reviewed IOS crown surface;
   - topology-only closure of only the new local crown opening(s);
   - verification that those local boundaries are closed;
   - Exact Boolean subtraction with the selected full-resolution CBCT teeth;
   - disconnected Boolean-debris cleanup;
   - secondary scan-hole repair while preserving the principal basal opening;
   - automatic anatomy-locked basal closure using the existing DSG closed-model
     pipeline.
7. Only a verified manifold post-extraction IOS can continue to DSG guide design.

UI/KISS

The Alignment panel is now context-sensitive. In an immediate case it shows one
primary action at a time:

DETECT COMMON SURFACE -> APPLY EXTRACTION + CLOSE -> CONTINUE

If the Boolean succeeds but automatic closure fails, the button becomes
RETRY MODEL CLOSURE and reuses the Boolean result instead of repeating the
destructive extraction.

Safety

- Common-surface detection requires successful three-zone alignment validation.
- Source IOS and source CBCT teeth are never edited by the common-surface step.
- The local crown filler never receives the principal basal IOS opening.
- Branched/non-simple local extraction boundaries fail closed for manual review.
- A failed final closure never permits transition to guide design.
