DSG 9.2.36 - Controlled local blockout relief

The seating-audit workflow now includes a controlled local relief action.

Workflow

1. Calculate the blockout from the desired insertion view.
2. Review the teal display-only blockout surface and its seating audit.
3. If a limited contact zone needs more clearance, enter Edit Mode on
   DSG_Blockout and select its teal-region vertices.
4. Set Local relief and apply it. DSG advances only the selected vertices and
   their one-ring transition along the stored insertion axis.
5. DSG immediately repeats its algorithmic seating audit. Confirm the
   retentive model only when the audit passes and after clinician review.

Safety guarantees

- The source IOS never changes.
- The correction is axial only; a non-axial candidate fails the audit.
- The selected area is softened with a half-strength one-ring transition rather
  than a sharp step.
- The action participates in Blender Undo.

This feature is intended for conservative, clinician-directed contact relief.
It does not replace print/material calibration, fit verification on the model,
or clinical responsibility.
