DSG 9.1.3 - Clinical Context + Country-Aware MCP Guidance
==========================================================

New
---
- dsg.clinical_core: pure-Python normalization, hashing, guidance-topic discovery,
  missing-information analysis and sourced-result validation.
- dsg.clinical_context: Blender scene storage + UI + MCP bridge integration.
- Patient clinical context intentionally excludes direct identifier fields.
- Country-aware source registry under resources/clinical/source_registry.json.
- Spain source priority includes AEMPS/CIMA, SECOMCyC, SEPA, Consejo General de
  Dentistas and Ministerio de Sanidad.
- Extensible data-only country registry with GB, US, EU and international fallbacks.
- Unregistered countries trigger explicit live discovery of the country's medicine
  regulator, dental professional body, oral/maxillofacial society and national guidance.
- AEMPS CIMA REST lookup request descriptor for Spanish medication reconciliation.
- New MCP bridge operations:
  get_patient_clinical_context
  set_patient_clinical_context
  clear_patient_clinical_context
  get_clinical_source_registry
  build_medication_lookup_request
  build_clinical_guidance_request
  get_clinical_guidance_request
  apply_clinical_guidance_result
  get_clinical_guidance_result
  build_preoperative_context
- build_full_implant_context now includes clinical status summary.
- Patient/procedure request and returned clinical guidance are cryptographically bound
  with request_sha256 to reject stale guidance after clinical data changes.
- Sourced clinical guidance claims must reference source IDs. At least one source is
  mandatory. The addon stores guidance as advisory evidence, not as automatic clearance.
- Clinical review confirmation is deliberately clinician-only in Blender UI and is not
  exposed as an MCP mutation tool.

Safety / architecture
---------------------
- DSG does not hard-code changing drug-holiday, antibiotic, anticoagulant, MRONJ or
  other medical treatment rules.
- MCP must perform live current-country verification before advising on delicate cases.
- Medication lookup requests send the drug term only; they do not transmit patient context.
- Geometry remains deterministic in DSG; medical interpretation remains sourced and
  auditable in MCP; final clinical decision remains with the treating clinician.
