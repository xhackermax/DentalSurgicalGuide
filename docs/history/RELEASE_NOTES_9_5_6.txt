DSG 9.5.6 - FDI Component Review Fix

- Fixes the false fatal gate when TotalSegmentator reports more than one significant island for a tooth label.
- Keeps the existing safety behavior: only the dominant connected island is materialized as the clinical tooth mesh.
- FDI duplicates and invalid FDI numbers remain hard errors.
- Multi-island labels now require/record the clinician's explicit visual FDI confirmation instead of cancelling the workflow.
- Stores the acknowledged FDI list and review count in the Blender Scene for audit/debugging.
- Resets the acknowledgement automatically whenever a new dentition is built.
- Updates the FDI panel to show whether the disconnected-island warning is pending or clinically reviewed.
