DSG 9.5.7 - HIGH-ORDER DENTAL GEOMETRY FIX
==============================================

Problem
- TotalSegmentator task=teeth was fast and preserved FDI identity, but its final
  labelmap could show staircase/block artefacts after model-space -> CBCT-space
  upsampling. VTK then faithfully converted those steps into a LEGO-like mesh.

Fix
- Enable TotalSegmentator 2.18 higher_order_resampling=True for task=teeth.
- Keep resampling_order=1 for fast input interpolation.
- Apply the same high-order output path on CUDA and on CPU fallback.
- Keep one neural inference only. No second dental model is added.
- Keep VTK native-grid extraction and all FDI/island safety checks unchanged.

Why this is preferable to Blender smoothing
- The smoother boundary is produced while TotalSegmentator upsamples its
  segmentation output, before triangulation.
- DSG does not free-sculpt or Laplacian-smooth the clinical tooth mesh after
  the fact. Contacts, roots and FDI identity remain tied to the model output.

Expected trade-off
- Slightly more post-inference runtime and memory than standard upsampling.
- Neural inference speed remains essentially the same; the added cost is in the
  final segmentation upsampling/export stage.

Retained from 9.5.6
- Disconnected secondary islands are cleaned but no longer block clinician FDI
  confirmation when the dominant tooth component is valid.
- Duplicate/invalid FDI remains a hard safety error.
