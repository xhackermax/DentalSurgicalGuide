DSG 9.6.3 - ORGANIC FRAME + USER-PLACED SLEEVE WINDOW

FRAME
- Replaces the final faceted POLY sweep with a continuous AUTO Bezier sweep.
- Keeps the solved geodesic surface route as the clinical source.
- Reduces only redundant display controls (about 0.8-1.45 mm spacing).
- Raises radial/longitudinal curve resolution for a smoother, more anatomical frame.
- Stores visible-sweep metadata on DSG_Frame for debugging/audit.

LATERAL SLEEVE WINDOW
- Removes automatic final placement of the lateral drill-entry window.
- After irrigation is resolved, the clinician clicks the curved sleeve wall on the side with the best drill access.
- Supports one manual pick per implant/sleeve and shows an in-viewport arrow marker.
- The opening width remains fixed to the inner sleeve diameter.
- Fine rotation remains available after the manual pick.
- If irrigation exists, DSG validates the picked side against the protected irrigation sector and refuses unsafe placement instead of auto-rotating or narrowing the slot.
- Applying the opening still cuts sleeve + underlying frame in one EXACT Boolean.

COMPATIBILITY
- Legacy operators are preserved so older .blend files/keymaps still register.
