DSG v9.2.88 — Segmentation Recovery Baseline

Purpose:
Restore a known-good clinical segmentation path before attempting further speed
work. The multi-process experiments from 9.2.73-9.2.87 are bypassed for active
segmentation.

Immediate route is now deliberately sequential:
1. DentalSegmentator on the proven in-process background worker thread.
2. Compact dental/canal verifier masks and ROI are retained.
3. UniversalLab runs next on the same proven worker architecture.
4. Tooth Path + full-resolution tooth mesh preparation uses the known-good
   v9.2.72 single background postprocess thread.
5. Blender commits prepared teeth, maxilla, mandible and canal incrementally.

Disabled/bypassed for segmentation:
- persistent AI server
- second-Blender isolated inference worker
- dual DentalSegmentator + UniversalLab process orchestration
- multi-process postprocess workers

Experimental workers are explicitly cancelled before starting IMMEDIATE.

No clinical model, FDI rule, CBCT resolution, immediate extraction workflow,
alignment workflow or final animation is removed. This is a reliability rollback
of the segmentation execution architecture only.

Once this baseline is confirmed to segment correctly again, performance can be
reintroduced one worker at a time with a measurable benchmark after each step.
