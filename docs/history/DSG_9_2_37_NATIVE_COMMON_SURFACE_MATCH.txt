DSG 9.2.37 - Native common-surface selection for immediate extraction

Scope

The immediate-implant workflow now creates the initial IOS crown selection by
matching the aligned IOS against only the CBCT teeth chosen for extraction.
This is the pre-Boolean selection used to prepare the virtual post-extraction
IOS; it is not a registration tool and it does not alter clinical geometry.

Method

- bounded IOS-to-CBCT BVH proximity;
- reciprocal CBCT-to-IOS verification, accepting the same or an adjacent IOS
  face only;
- two-sided normal compatibility for open/cropped scanner surfaces;
- coverage-weighted face confidence;
- connected, anchored-region filtering and conservative continuity growth;
- persisted per-face confidence attribute: DSG_common_surface_confidence.

Workflow

1. Complete and review IOS-CBCT alignment.
2. Choose the tooth/teeth for extraction.
3. Press DETECT COMMON SURFACE.
4. DSG enters Edit Mode on a disposable IOS copy with the matched region
   selected. The panel reports selected-face count and P95 match distance.
5. Refine only locally with Ctrl+ until the cervical groove/contact boundary is
   clinically appropriate, then confirm the post-extraction IOS.

Safety

- IOS, CBCT teeth and their registration are never moved or scaled.
- A missing/displaced CBCT reference produces no usable selection and stops the
  workflow.
- The selected faces remain clinician-reviewable and editable before any
  destructive Boolean operation.

Provenance

The workflow requirement was inspired by GeoMatch Fusion 0.3.5 supplied by the
user. GeoMatch Fusion is GPL-3.0-or-later; no source code from it is bundled in
DSG. DSG uses an independently implemented native matcher.
