DSG 9.1.18 — Alignment clinical surface review

Problem fixed:
The previous alignment review hid native-resolution segmented teeth and showed
Dental_DICOM_AlignmentRef, a deliberately downsampled shell (max_axis ~176)
created only for fast ICP. This looked like the segmentation had lost geometry.

New behavior:
- ICP still uses the lightweight shell internally.
- the shell is hidden during review when individual segmented teeth exist.
- native-resolution UniversalLab tooth meshes are shown instead.
- a temporary non-destructive Laplacian Smooth modifier (2 iterations, low
  strength, volume preservation when supported) improves surface readability.
- FDI labels remain visible on the clinical teeth.
- the smoothing modifier is disabled before entering Surgical Guide.
- the original clinical mesh datablock is never voxel-remeshed or overwritten.
- the no-AI DICOM STL route retains the original fallback behavior.
